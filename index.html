Put them on your Home Screen

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Open on an app above.
  3. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  4. Launch it from the Home Screen. It runs full screen, like a real app.
  5. Repeat for the other app.

Balances, cards and transactions are simulated. Nothing here is real money or a real payment card.
